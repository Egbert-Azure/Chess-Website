Hoogeveen, October 2011. Vladimir Kramnik had White against Anish Giri. King’s Indian, the Bayonet: 9.b4. After nine moves their board showed exactly the position on mine against Matthew Nohr, move for move. Kramnik played 10.g3 and won in 35 moves. I played 10.Re1. My note on 9.b4 at the time: “pretty much common theory.”

Electronic Knights 2021 Final, US Chess, no engines. Before move 28 I noted: “I thought about the next move for a pretty long time …” My game lasted 54 moves.
