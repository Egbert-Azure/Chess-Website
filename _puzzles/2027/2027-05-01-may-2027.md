Thirty years ago this month, on 11 May 1997, Garry Kasparov resigned the sixth game against IBM’s Deep Blue after 19 moves. The game lasted about an hour. The machine won the match, two wins to one, with three draws.

I lost to a human. Electronic Knights 2021 Final, no engines on either side. Tim Corkum had Black. We had met once before, in the 2021 Semi-Final, and drawn. My note on that game: “I had the feeling I was leading, but Tim was able to turn the tide and managed a draw brilliantly. An honor to play with him.”

This time, on move 16 I noted: “I don't feel comfortable and have a hard time calculating a plan.” And on 37.Re8, my move just before this position: “That was a blunder and after this Black is on a winning path.” Corkum found the answer himself. A brilliant player and always fun to chat with. Can't wait to hear or read his Sláinte again.

We met a third time in the RoW Semi-Final, with engines: another draw.
