Aron Nimzowitsch, in *My System*: “The passed Pawn is a criminal, who should be kept under lock and key.”

ICCF Champions League 2021, a team event. My team was called The Underrateds. Matthew Tedesco played for The Poisoned Pawns. I had Black. By move 48 three white pawns stood on the sixth rank, and my rook and knight were the lock and key. My notes call 36...Rcd8 the move that lost. I can't remember exactly, but it was a blunder.
