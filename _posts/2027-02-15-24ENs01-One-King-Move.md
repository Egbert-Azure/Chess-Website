## The tournament

Electronic Knights 2024 Semi-Final, section 24ENs01. US Chess, on the ICCF server, no engines. Seven players, six games each. It started on 31 May 2025. The games count for the US Chess correspondence rating, not for the ICCF rating.

## My result

3 out of 6: one win, against Benjamin Hoback, four draws and one loss. 5th of 7. John C. Walton won the section with 4½.

## My opponent

John C. Walton, USA, CCM, the top seed: ICCF rating 2421 when the section started; mine was 2180. US Chess correspondence rating 2367 when this game was rated; mine was 2137. Our first game.

## The game

I had Black. King's Indian Defence, Fianchetto Variation, Classical Main Line. 9...Qb6 was the first small step wrong; the engine prefers 9...exd4. White answered 10.c5!, and after 10...dxc5 11.dxe5 Ne8 12.e6 fxe6 13.Ng5, a pawn given for the attack, he had the initiative: about −1 for me (1).

I held that for almost fifty moves. 28...b3 made it a little worse (−1.35). By move 60 we had a queen ending, and I was a pawn down (−2.0). Then 60...Kd8? allowed 61.Qb6+: the queens come off, and after 61...Qxb6 62.axb6 the pawn ending is lost (2, 3). 60...Kf8 would have kept the queens on. I resigned.

## Why I lost

Two moments, according to the engine. The opening: after 9...Qb6 White got 10.c5 and an initiative that lasted the whole game. And the end: after fifty moves of defence, one king move to the wrong side.
