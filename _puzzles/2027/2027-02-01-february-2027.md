The tournament carried a name: Victor Palciauskas. Born in Kaunas, Lithuania, in 1941. Physics professor in California. From 1978 to 1984 the tenth World Correspondence Chess Champion. I didn't know him, nor had I heard of him. Now I proudly hold the signed document as the undisputed winner 🏆 of the tournament. It was the first tournament I ever signed up for, and my first tournament win. Counts triple at my age.

Bruce Moskowitz had White. US Chess, no engines. By move 16 his king had walked to e2, and my queen stood on b5. I wrote about this game once before: “A later analysis showed that move 15. Ke2 loses the game for White. However, a win needs to be carried home.” I won the tournament 6–0; in August 2021 US Chess’s column “Check Is In The Mail” listed it among the recent event winners.
