## Welcome back

Now this is the first blog post since April, and it's time for an update. As said in my last blog, I reduced the amount of tournaments for the good and focused on Electronic Knights. This gives me a USCF correspondence ranking but the tournaments are hosted on ICCF engine free. Even signing up for just 1 board I actually play 3 tournaments in parallel. I lost my team for Champions League “The Underrateds” because most retired from competitive chess.

So, in the next months I give some updates on interesting games and some thoughts.

## The tournament

Electronic Knights 2025 Preliminary, section 25EN03. Electronic Knights is US Chess's correspondence championship, now played on the ICCF server and without engines. Seven players, six games each. It started on 5 March 2025. The games count for the US Chess correspondence rating, not for the ICCF rating.

## My result

5 out of 6: five wins and one loss, against Brent Walker. Shared first place with Jerry Weiner, also on 5 points. His only loss was the game below. That put me in the 2025 Semi-Final, which started on 31 August 2026.

In April I wrote: “Currently tied for 1st place with 5 points alongside Jerry Weiner.” That is how it finished.

## My opponent

Jerry Weiner (Gerald H. Weiner in the US Chess lists), USA. ICCF rating 2157 when the section started; mine was 2175. US Chess correspondence rating 2201 when this game was rated; mine was 2123. Not our first game: in the 2021 Final, which started in January 2024, I held a draw against him with Black. We meet a third time in the 2025 Semi-Final, still running.

## The game

I had Black. Pirc Defence: 1.e4 d6 2.d4 Nf6 3.Nc3 g6 4.f3 c6 5.Be3 b5. White castled long on move 7; I waited until move 20. {% comment %}Your part: why the Pirc against Weiner, and why ...b5 so early?{% endcomment %}

No single mistake decided this game. From move 20 the engine has me slightly better (+0.4), and the edge grew in small steps. 25...a4 and 26...a3 put a pawn deep in White's camp (1). 29...Rd1+ gave my two rooks for his queen (2). From move 37 it was my queen against his two rooks, five pawns each, and my e- and f-pawns ran. Weiner resigned after 54...Qg4 (3).

## Why I won

The engine finds no big mistake on either side (not sure if it counts but hey, I take it. For sure such a judgment is better than those bogus ChatGPT annotations from ChessBase): White's worst move cost less than a pawn (49.b4). Small advantages added up, the kind of game that is possible without engines. {% comment %}Your part: when did you feel it was won?
