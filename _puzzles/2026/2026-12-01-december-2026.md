Five years ago this month, on 3 December 2021 in Dubai, Magnus Carlsen and Ian Nepomniachtchi played 136 moves. Seven hours and 45 minutes. The longest game in the history of the World Championship. Carlsen won.

My game against Mark McMahon was shorter. Electronic Knights 2025, US Chess, played without engines. On move 15 I gave up my rook: 15.axb5 Qxa1 16.bxc6, and the pawn ran on to c7. After 20 moves it was over. The position was all about calculating and setting up the trap. What if he takes the bait and takes the rook? He couldn't resist and made a quick move. That was it. The trap snapped.
